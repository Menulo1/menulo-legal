Menulo Privacy Policy

Menulo is a personal dining companion for recording restaurant visits, order variants, taste notes, and optional AI-assisted drafts. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Menulo iOS application.

Information stored on your device

Menulo is designed primarily as a local app. The following information may be saved in the app sandbox or device preferences:

  • Visit records you create, including venue names, channels (dine-in, pickup, delivery), order lines, ratings, and free-form taste notes
  • Photos you attach to visits, including camera captures and images selected from your photo library
  • Voice transcription drafts created from microphone input and on-device speech recognition
  • Gift pack purchase records and remaining AI assist credits
  • Local moderation choices for demo Circle content, such as blocked authors
  • Simple app state flags such as whether you have completed the guide screen

Permissions

Menulo requests sensitive permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — to photograph menus, receipts, or dish references, and for menu scan drafts
  • Photo Library — to choose existing images for visit attachments or feedback-style media
  • Microphone — to capture short voice taste notes
  • Speech Recognition — to convert those voice notes into editable text

Menulo does not use App Tracking Transparency identifiers and does not implement advertising tracking. Menulo does not register a notification center delegate for push delivery in the A-side experience described by this policy.

In-app purchases

Gift packs are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Menulo stores product identifiers and unlock state locally so AI assist credits can be granted after a successful purchase. Apple processes payment details under Apple Media Services terms. Menulo does not receive your full payment card number.

Network activity

Core journaling can work offline. Certain features may use Apple system services, including speech recognition and App Store product lookups. Opening Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads hosted legal pages inside the app. Menulo does not operate a proprietary backend for uploading your private visit notes as part of the default local experience.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If you choose to share a taste card or exported content through the system share sheet, the destination is under your control.

Retention and deletion

You can clear local data from Settings. Logging out returns you to the guide screen without requiring a cloud account. Uninstalling Menulo removes sandbox data, subject to any device backups you keep.

Children

Menulo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about Menulo, use the support contact listed on the Menulo App Store product page.
